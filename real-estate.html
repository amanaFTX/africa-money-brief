How AMB checks property data

  • Grades: A official or transaction index, B published research report, C listing-portal asking sample (needs at least 20 listings), D crowdsourced.
  • Gates: gross yield 3–15%, rent × 12 ÷ price must match the published yield, anything stale is labelled, and suspect figures are held out.
  • Johannesburg: City average 13.47% (GPG) is consistent with Gauteng sectional-title returns of just over 12% (PayProp/TPN, 2025).
  • Cape Town: City average 9.49% (GPG) is consistent with Western Cape sectional-title yields below 10% (PayProp/TPN, 2025).
  • Not investment advice. Property is illiquid; published yields are gross and based on asking prices.

Held out of this release

  • Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana pages from a yield publisher: Last updated 2005, 2016 and 2020 respectively; stale.
  • Cairo Mohandessin 2-bed (12.03%): Price identical to the New Cairo studio and below its own 3-bed; likely a data error.
  • Johannesburg 2-bed (15.16%): Above the 15% yield gate; held for verification.
  • Accra: Only sponsored-content estimates and appreciation rates; no verified yield.
  • Abuja, Kigali: No current published yield found.
