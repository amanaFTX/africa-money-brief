How we calculate

  • Premium = (parallel buying midpoint ÷ CBN selling rate − 1) × 100: what you gain by buying at the CBN’s selling rate and selling to a parallel-market operator at their buying rate.
  • Official rates: the latest published Central Bank of Nigeria figures, with the date shown. USD is the CBN NFEM rate (equal to the CBN’s selling rate). GBP and EUR are derived when the CBN’s rows lag: CBN NFEM × the ECB reference cross for the same day, checked against the CBN’s own rows (flagged if more than 0.3% apart).
  • AMB midpoint is informational: the average of the buy-range and sell-range midpoints.
  • Wide-range flag when a quote range exceeds the larger of ₦20 or 1.5% of the midpoint; quotes failing outlier checks are held back.
  • Freshness badges show days since each official rate’s effective date.
  • Sources: official rates cite the benchmark; parallel observations are aggregated from multiple independent market sources, not named individually.
