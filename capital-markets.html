DANGOTE REFINERY IPO • 7 OCT 2026

Six days to scheduled close

Africa's largest share sale is widening beyond Nigeria as East African access develops.

OFFER TERMS

₦525 per share • 4.1bn shares

The SEC-approved offer opened 14 September and is scheduled to close 13 October. Gross proceeds would be about ₦2.15tn if fully subscribed. Minimum Nigerian subscription: 10 shares (₦5,250). The offer proceeds support the refinery expansion programme.

UGANDA • NEW

Regulator clears participation

Uganda's CMA has approved access, with current marketing directed to high-net-worth and professional investors through authorised channels. This extends the IPO's cross-border reach.

KENYA • GDR

Cross-border route opens

Kenya's CMA approved a short-form prospectus for a GDR structure. The proposed GDR listing remains subject to required Nigerian approvals, fundraising and allocation of the underlying shares.

RETAIL AMBITION

Target: 10 million retail investors

Refinery CEO David Bird says the IPO aims to attract 10 million retail investors—more than twice Saudi Aramco's 2019 retail subscriber count. That is an ambition, not evidence that the target has been reached.

AMB TAKEAWAY

Positive: widening regional access strengthens the pan-African distribution story. Still unanswered: AMB has not found authoritative public subscription/allotment data showing how much of the ₦2.15tn offer has actually been subscribed. The ₦525 investment case therefore still rests on the prospectus fundamentals, execution of the expansion plan, operating performance and eventual market pricing—not headline retail-interest targets alone.

INVESTOR SAFETY

Nigeria's SEC says applications and payments should use only officially designated receiving agents and approved subscription channels. Investors should verify platforms and avoid anyone guaranteeing preferential allotment.
