## Formula controls

- AMB midpoint = (parallel buy-range midpoint + parallel sell-range midpoint) / 2.
- Parallel-buy premium (%) = (parallel buy-range midpoint / CBN selling rate − 1) × 100.
- Premiums are rounded to two decimal places only after calculation.
