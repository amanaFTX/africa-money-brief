## Nigeria parallel validation

Five current independent observations were reviewed for each currency. The final ranges deliberately preserve material same-day cross-source dispersion:

| Currency | Buy range | Sell range | Buy midpoint | Sell midpoint | AMB midpoint | Premium vs CBN selling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| USD | 1365–1375 | 1375–1390 | 1370.00 | 1382.50 | 1376.25 | +2.81% |
| GBP | 1800–1850 | 1820–1880 | 1825.00 | 1850.00 | 1837.50 | +3.37% |
| EUR | 1500–1550 | 1520–1590 | 1525.00 | 1555.00 | 1540.00 | +2.24% |

Premium formula: `(parallel buying midpoint / CBN selling rate - 1) × 100`.

Public attribution is preserved exactly: “Aggregated from multiple independent market sources.” Provider names remain in validator evidence only, not in the public package.
