AMB ₦10M TAKEAWAY

For a ₦10m balance, published savings products verified so far span roughly 8.25%–9.45% p.a. The latest 91-day NTB stop rate is materially higher at 15.50%, but liquidity, tax, pricing basis and access differ.

AMB TAKEAWAY

Do not compare percentages blindly

The first board already shows why product type and liquidity matter as much as the headline rate.

Nigeria: recent NTB rates cluster around 15.5–15.9%, creating a useful benchmark for bank deposits and money-market funds.

Ghana: the August banking-system 6-month deposit average (9.72%) exceeded the latest 182-day bill interest rate (6.4894%).

Kenya: recent T-bill rates around 8.8–9.0% exceed August system savings/deposit averages.

Egypt: ~24–25% secondary T-bill yields lead this board nominally, but cross-country comparisons require inflation and FX context.
